## blocklist-check

### Problem

Site owners find out their mail or site is blocked because of their hosting provider's reputation, not their own, and only by accident. A Tell HN thread (100 points, 59 comments) describes it:

> "I recently discovered by accident that our website was being blocked by Orange's security filters. After a quick check, I found that our IP address is listed on UCEPROTECT Level 3. The listing is based on the reputation of the entire ASN 14061 (DigitalOcean, US)... even if your IP did nothing wrong, it will still be listed."

The same post says the whitelist costs about $30/month or $108/year. The pain is that nobody checks, so the owner is the last to know. Only one thread supports this, and the author had already run a check by hand, so a checker is not what they asked for. A lookup tool is my guess at what would help. The thread's comments are not in the export, so I can't say how many people describe their own case.

### Target users

Small site owners and self-hosters on shared cloud ASNs (DigitalOcean and similar), plus people running their own mail servers. Reach: the Hacker News thread itself, r/selfhosted and r/sysadmin, and the self-hosted email community. The Lobsters ask "Email Self Hosters - what are you using?" (https://lobste.rs/s/rwloew/email_self_hosters_what_are_you_using) shows the audience exists, but it states no pain about blocklists. Size of the first 100: unknown.

### MVP in one day

- One input: an IPv4 address (or a hostname resolved to one).
- A Worker endpoint runs DNS-over-HTTPS lookups against a fixed list of public DNSBL zones, including the UCEPROTECT levels 1–3, and returns listed or not for each.
- The page shows one hairline-divided row per list: name, result, and what that level means (level 3 means the whole ASN is listed, not the IP).
- Shareable result URL with the query in the path. No accounts and no storage in v1.
- Empty and loading states; works at 320px, both themes.

### Existing alternatives

- Multi-list blocklist checkers exist (MXToolbox and similar), but the competition file has no matching launch or project (searched for dnsbl, blocklist, blacklist, uceprotect, reputation). I have no evidence from the signals on how well they cover UCEPROTECT level 3 or explain ASN-wide listings, so the gap is unknown.
- Checking by hand with `dig` against the list zones: it works, but needs the zone names and the reversed-IP format.
