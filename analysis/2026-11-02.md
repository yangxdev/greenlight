## cold-catalog

### Problem

People who keep media and backups on external or offline drives can't see what is on a drive without plugging it in. One poster putting family media into long-term cold storage asks for exactly this: "are there any tools out there that allow me to browse which files are in cold storage, without the actual drives being attached / mounted? So only info like file location, name, metadata, perhaps icon, etc." (self-hosting community on Lemmy, 36 points / 18 comments, 2026-10-28).

A second, adjacent case: a Plex user whose drive crashed has the library's metadata on another drive but cannot get a list of what the movies were: "I am having trouble getting a list of movie titles that are on my Plex… I have not been able to find the list, or the location of the list, so I can export it out and start the process of re-adding movies." (Plex forum, 0 points / 2 replies, 2026-10-29). This is the same need seen after a failure: a plain list of what a drive held. It is one person's support question, so it counts as supporting evidence only, not as a second request for the tool.

How often: unknown. Two posts in one week from two communities.

### Target users

Self-hosters and home-lab people who rotate backup or archive drives: r/selfhosted, the selfhosted communities on Lemmy, r/DataHoarder, and the Plex and Jellyfin forums. Whether any of them allows link posts is unknown.

### MVP

- The user picks a folder or drive in the browser (File System Access API, Chromium only) and the app walks it locally. Nothing is uploaded.
- It stores a snapshot per drive: path, file name, size, modified date, extension. The snapshot is saved in the browser (IndexedDB) and can be exported or imported as a JSON or CSV file, so it survives without an account.
- One screen lists the saved drives and searches across all of them by name, extension or size ("which drive has `2019-wedding.mkv`").
- Each drive entry has a label and a "last scanned" date. Re-scanning shows what was added or removed since the last snapshot.
- Zero sign-up. The Worker only serves the static app.

- Desktop disk catalogers (for example Cathy, gWhere, DiskCatalogMaker, WinCatalog): not verified in the signals or the competition file. From general knowledge they are desktop apps tied to one operating system and mostly old or paid. The Critic should check them.
- The poster in the Lemmy thread does not say which tools they tried. Replies are not in the export: unknown.

### Signals / sources

## shop-margin

### Problem

Small online shop owners can't tell what they really earn per order, because fees and refunds live in different places than the sales. A Shopify seller who also sells on TikTok Shop writes: "TikTok orders sync into Shopify, but the fees don't. The commission, affiliate, FBT, and shipping charges only live in TikTok's settlement reports. So Shopify reports show TikTok as more profitable than it is. How are you handling this? Spreadsheets, an app, or just looking at payouts?" (Shopify community, 2 points / 2 replies, 2026-10-28).

A second thread asks the general version: "Between ad spend, product costs, shipping, payment fees, and refunds, do you have a clear picture of what you're actually making? Or are you still pulling numbers from different tools and spreadsheets?" (Shopify community, 6 / 5, 2026-10-28). A third asks about refunds: "I'm exploring a simple tool to help Shopify stores calculate refunds when a customer returns only some items from a discounted order. How do you handle this now, and is it a problem you run into often?" (Shopify community, 3 / 2, 2026-10-26).

Caveat: the second and third posts read like people researching a product to build, not sellers describing their own pain. Only the first states a concrete gap in the poster's own words. The replies are not in the export, so whether sellers confirmed the pain is unknown. How often: unknown.

### Target users

Small Shopify and TikTok Shop sellers who keep a spreadsheet: the Shopify community forum, r/shopify, r/ecommerce and r/TikTokShop. Whether these allow link posts is unknown. The Shopify forum has a section for apps and tools, which is unconfirmed.

### MVP

- Paste or drop two CSV exports in the browser: the shop's orders and the marketplace settlement report (TikTok Shop first). Parsing happens in the browser; nothing is stored on a server.
- A column-mapping step, saved in the browser, so a changed export format can be fixed by the user without a code change.
- One result table per order: sale, product cost (entered once per product or as a percent), marketplace fees, shipping, refunds, margin. Orders with no matching settlement row are flagged.
- A total line and a download of the table as CSV.
- Partial-refund helper: a small calculator that splits a discount across items for a partial return. This is the single-purpose utility from the third thread.

### Existing alternatives

- Competition file: grepped for profit, TikTok, refund and settlement. No matching launch or project (the hits were unrelated).
- Shopify profit-tracking apps: not verified in the signals. From general knowledge there are several paid apps that take ad spend and use store logins. The Critic should check whether any supports TikTok Shop settlement files and what they cost.
- A free VAT calculator for Shopify sellers was posted in the same forum on 2026-10-31 (https://community.shopify.com/t/free-vat-calculator-for-31-european-countries-reliable-rates-for-shopify-sellers/707275). It is a neighbouring free tool, which shows the forum accepts them, and it is not competition for this problem.
