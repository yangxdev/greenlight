## home-reach-check

### Problem

People who self-host a media server or home service at home cannot tell why it is "not available outside your network". Three separate Plex forum threads in one week describe the same dead end: ports forwarded, UPnP tried, and still no remote access, with no way to see which layer is broken.

- "I'm getting seriously p***d off with Plex deciding it just doesnt want to allow remote access. It's a clean install… you can see I have port forwarded set on my router" (Plex forum, 3 replies): https://forums.plex.tv/t/getting-angry-with-remote-access/944679
- "In the Remote Access setting, however, it says 'Not available outside your network'… I have manually specify public port checked" (Plex forum, 3 replies): https://forums.plex.tv/t/problem-with-accessing-plex-on-my-nas-from-other-clients/944762
- "remote access continually flaps… Checks from outside network show its reachable… Plex App on same Phone shows server offline" (Plex forum, 2 replies): https://forums.plex.tv/t/published-mapping-state-response-was-422/944545
- Adjacent, a different community: "my public IP seems to change regularly every 2-3 weeks… my services keep being taken down from my IP changing" and the ISP won't give a static IP without a business plan (Lemmy c/selfhosted, 34 points / 48 comments): https://lemmy.dbzer0.com/post/77010890

Honest limit: each Plex thread is one person with 0-1 score and 2-3 replies, so this is repeated but low-engagement pain. The Lemmy post is the only one with real engagement, and it is adjacent (dynamic IP), not the same failure.

### Target users

Home Plex, Jellyfin and other self-hosters who forward a port and it does not work. Communities: r/PleX, r/selfhosted, r/homelab, the Plex forum, c/selfhosted on Lemmy. The owner can post a free tool in these. Whether they accept tool posts is unknown (not checked).

### MVP

- One page, no sign-up. The Worker reads the visitor's public IP from the request and shows it.
- Visitor pastes the WAN IP shown in their router (or Plex's "Public IP"). If it differs from the IP the Worker sees, the page says "carrier-grade NAT or double NAT, port forwarding cannot work" and lists the usual ways out.
- Visitor picks a port (default 32400). The Worker tries a TCP connection to the visitor's own IP on that port only (never a user-supplied host) and reports open, closed or timed out, with a one-line meaning of each.
- Static checklist per result (UPnP vs manual rule, "manually specify public port", router vs OS firewall, dynamic IP), written in plain language.
- One key action for Web Analytics: "Run check" clicked. Open question: whether Workers' outbound TCP sockets can reach arbitrary home IPs from the free plan is unverified and needs a spike before the blueprint.

### Existing alternatives

- Generic open-port checkers (the canyouseeme-style sites): not checked this week and not in the competition file, so their quality is unknown. From general knowledge they only say open or closed and do not detect double NAT or explain the next step.
- Plex's own Remote Access settings page: shows "Not available outside your network" with no cause, which is what the three threads complain about.
- Competition file: no match for port forwarding, remote access or self-hosting tunnels (the only "plex" hits were the word "complex").

### Explicit non-goals

- No scanning of any host other than the visitor's own IP, and no port ranges.
- No dynamic DNS, tunnels or account system.
- No Plex login or reading of the visitor's server.
- No router-specific guides in v1.
